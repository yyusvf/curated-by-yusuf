What is this?

My curated collection of Ye and others' edits, put together in playlists by me — featuring noteworthy compilations from the community.

Most of what you see here comes from the Yedits community, a group of fans who take unreleased, unfinished, or poorly mixed Kanye music and finish it themselves.

Everything is playable directly via Samply, a free music app available on iOS, Mac, and as a web app on Windows. All editors are fully credited.

Join the Yedits Discord or check out the trackers linked below for more.

Get the comps on your music player

Everything on Samply can be downloaded as normal audio files and imported into any music app. Download first, then open the section for your player.

💡 Easiest on a PC — once the songs are in your library, they sync to your phone automatically while Spotify (or Apple Music) is open on both devices on the same network.

1

Download from Samply

Open the comp in Samply and use the download option to save the audio files to your device — the Files app on iPhone/iPad, or your Downloads folder on a computer.

Spotify — Desktop ›

1. Click your profile picture at the top and select Settings.

2. Scroll to Library and switch Show Local Files on.

3. Under Show songs from, turn on the folders you want — or click Add a source to select the folder with your downloads.

4. Go to Your Library → Collection. Your music now appears under Local Files.

Spotify — Mobile ›

1. Tap your profile picture at the top.

2. Tap Settings and privacy → Settings.

3. Tap Apps and devices.

4. Switch Local audio files on.

5. Go to Your Library → Collection. Your music now appears under Local Files.

Adding files on iPhone

In the Files app, open the folder of your browser / storage app, select the file and choose Copy or Move.

Tap Browse → On My iPhone, open the Spotify folder, then choose Paste / Move item here.

Apple Music — with subscription ›

Step 1 · Add files to your desktop library

Open the Music (or iTunes) app on your computer.

Drag your local audio files into the window, or use File → Import.

Optional: right-click a track and choose Get Info to edit Artist, Album or Artwork so your library stays tidy.

Step 2 · Turn on Sync Library

Make sure Sync Library (iCloud Music Library) is on in your desktop app settings.

Turn on Sync Library on your iPhone/iPad too, under Settings → Music.

Your songs upload to the cloud and appear on your other devices within a few minutes.

Apple Music — without subscription ›

1. Add the music to your computer — import your MP3s into the Music app (Mac) or the Apple Music / iTunes app (Windows) as usual.

2. Connect your device via USB. On macOS, open Finder and click your iPhone under "Locations". On Windows, open the Apple Devices app (or iTunes) and select your device.

3. Start a manual sync — open the Music tab, tick "Sync music onto [your device]", choose your whole library or selected playlists / artists / albums, then click Apply / Sync.

🎵 Any other player ›

The downloads are standard MP3 files — import them into VLC, foobar2000, the Files app, or any app that plays local audio.
